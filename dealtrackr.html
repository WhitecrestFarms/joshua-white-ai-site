How to complete and import a template

1. DownloadChoose the template that matches the information you want to import.
2. Fill it outKeep the first row and column names exactly as provided. Add one record per row and leave unused cells blank.
3. Import in DealTrackrOpen Agent Settings, choose Import / Export Data, select the matching import type, and choose your completed CSV file.
DatesUse yyyy-MM-dd, such as 2026-02-28.
MoneyEnter plain numbers only—no dollar signs or commas. Example: 250000.
PercentagesEnter a whole number. Use 3 for 3%.
Multiple tagsSeparate tags with a semicolon or comma. Example: Buyer; Past Client.
Referral Is %Enter Yes or No.
Blank cellsBlank cells are allowed and will be left unused.

Why I Built DealTrackr

Created from the daily reality of working in real estate.

As an active REALTOR®, I wanted a straightforward way to manage dates, contacts, notes, follow-ups, and production without building my business around an oversized software platform.

DealTrackr was designed to feel useful immediately: simple enough for one agent, organized enough for a growing pipeline, and flexible enough to support the way each professional works.
