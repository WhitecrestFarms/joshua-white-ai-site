Why I Built DealTrackr

Created from the daily reality of working in real estate.

As an active REALTOR®, I wanted a straightforward way to manage dates, contacts, notes, follow-ups, and production without building my business around an oversized software platform.

DealTrackr was designed to feel useful immediately: simple enough for one agent, organized enough for a growing pipeline, and flexible enough to support the way each professional works.

Built by an agent, for agents.
