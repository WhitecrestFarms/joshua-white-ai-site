Built by a REALTOR®, Not a Software Company

Every feature starts with a real problem.

I never set out to become an app developer. I became frustrated by tools that were expensive, overly complicated, or simply did not fit the way homeowners and real estate professionals actually work.

Every feature inside HomeTrackr and DealTrackr comes from real experiences helping clients buy, sell, manage, and care for homes. My goal is the same whether I am representing a client or building an app: make the process simpler, more organized, and less stressful.

Honest advice. Smart technology. Better experiences.
